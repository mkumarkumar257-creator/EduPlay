ABOUT THE PROJECT

Learning with a little more play.

EduPlay is a gamified learning experience platform being developed as an MCA academic project. Its goal is to make learning feel engaging, personal, and rewarding.

LEARN · PLAY · LEVEL UP

A platform designed to grow in clear stages.

The first release establishes the shared experience: a responsive public site, secure accounts for learners, and a private workspace. The next phases can add learning activities, educator tools, and progress features on top of that foundation.
